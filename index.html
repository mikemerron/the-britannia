18th century bones, 21st century soul
The Bar

A historic Scarborough pub, revived for the present.

Stripped back to brick, stone and board, then put together with care: filament light, bentwood stools, bare timber floors. Nothing shouted, nothing fake.

The bar itself is bespoke — a sculptural reading of the sea wall that wraps Scarborough, drawn as one clean curve to set against the patchwork of historic stone and brick behind it.

Cocktails made properly, wine worth ordering by the glass, and cold beer poured without ceremony. Two rooms and a staircase, open from two in the afternoon.

The building is eighteenth century. Everything we do in it is for now.
